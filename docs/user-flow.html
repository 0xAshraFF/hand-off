Every screen, and where the flow splits in two

Six questions, one at a time. Then step 6 decides everything: skip it and the answer is computed instantly with no login and no cap, because structured answers plus a cached catalog is a pure function. Write in it and the flow crosses into sign-in, the daily quota, and a model call.

Metered pathsign-in · 3 per day · model call

The whole flow

Read top to bottom. The two coloured lanes are the only structural decision in the product — everything above them is shared, everything below them rejoins.

Where the catalog comes from

The recommendation is only a pure function because the numbers are already on disk when the request arrives. Nothing in the user flow above ever calls a benchmark API.

Screen by screen

What sits on each page, and what it leads to.

Screen 01

What are you building?

  • Six tiles, icon and one line each
  • Agent, chatbot, documents, coding tool, creative, something else
  • Selecting auto-advances — no Next button

Leads to screen 02. The tile sets which jobs appear in the results.

Screen 02

Where does it run?

  • Cloud, local, hybrid, or never leaves my machine
  • Drives a hard filter, not a preference

Leads to screen 03. Picking local removes every hosted model from scoring.

Screen 03

What does it handle?

  • Multi-select: text, images, PDFs, audio, video, code, long documents
  • Needs a confirm button, unlike the single-select screens

Leads to screen 04. Each modality adds a job card to the results.

Screen 04

What can you spend?

  • Slider from $0 to $500, month or run
  • Label reads in plain language, not numbers alone

Leads to screen 05. Sets the meter the results are measured against.

Screen 05

What matters most?

  • Three sliders totalling 100 — quality, speed, cost
  • Dragging one redistributes the others

Leads to screen 06. These are the weights in the scoring function.

Screen 06 · the fork

Describe it in your own words

  • Optional free text, hard cap of 150 words
  • Live counter: grey, amber at 130, red and blocking at 150
  • A visible Skip link, weighted equally to the submit button

Skip → the free path. Write → sign-in, quota, model call.

Free path

Scored on the spot

  • Deterministic scoring over the cached catalog
  • No sign-in, no quota, no model call
  • Renders immediately — no analyzing state to show

Leads straight to the results screen.

Metered path

Sign in, then analyze

  • Google sign-in on first visit only
  • Quota check — three runs a day
  • GLM-5.3-Flash, escalating to GLM-5.3 when the text is vague
  • Out of runs falls back to the free path, never a wall

Leads to the analyzing state, then results.

Screen 07 · metered path only

Analyzing

  • Real steps checking off one by one
  • Read your description · scored 340 models · matched jobs
  • Two to three seconds, then it resolves on its own

Leads to the results screen. Never shown on the free path.

Screen 08

Your results

  • One card per job, named in plain language
  • The pick, an underrated alternative, three more to compare
  • Every number captioned with which direction is good
  • Swapping a model recomputes the cost meter live

Leads to the handoff, or back up to any chip to re-answer.

Screen 09

Your handoff

  • Default tab is prose to paste into Claude or ChatGPT
  • AGENTS.md and models.json behind it
  • User text quoted and fenced, never merged into instructions

Leads to save or share, both optional and both off by default.

Side entrance

The leaderboard

  • Every model ranked, scored and priced
  • Reachable without entering the wizard at all
  • Static and cached — the cheapest page you serve

Leads anywhere — including into the wizard, if something catches their eye.

What a run on the metered path costs

Roughly 1,500 tokens in and 400 out, per run. The escalation tier is the only line that is not a rounding error, and it fires on about one run in five.

At three runs a day, 100,000 users cost about $333 a year in model calls. Vercel and Supabase together cost more than that before a single call is made. The quota is there to stop scripted abuse, not to protect a budget — so make it three per day, not three per lifetime.
