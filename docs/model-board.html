The Haiku ruleYou cannot build a quant engine on Claude Haiku 4.5. You also should not be paying frontier prices to tag ten thousand support emails — and for that, Haiku is close to ideal. It is not a worse model. It is a model for different work.

This is why nothing on this page is ranked. A ranking tells you which model is "best", which is the wrong question and quietly pushes everyone toward the most expensive answer. Fit tells you where each one shines and where it will let you down — and once you have that, the cheap models stop looking like compromises and start looking like the right tool for two thirds of what you actually do.
